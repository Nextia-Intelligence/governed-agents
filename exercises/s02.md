# Exercise 2: narrow the address

**Time:** about 15 minutes.

Sam's `change_address` tool accepts any text of 5 to 100 characters as an address. But an
address with a line break, or with `<` and `>` in it, is a warning sign: it can hide text
that the next reader (a person, or another model) takes as an instruction.

## Your task

In `src/bluekite/governance/registry.py`, narrow the `address` argument of `change_address`:

1. It is at most 80 characters long.
2. It has no line breaks, and no `<` or `>` characters.

Hint: the small validator in the same file already understands `pattern` and `maxLength`.
